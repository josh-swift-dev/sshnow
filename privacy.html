SSH Now Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

SSH Now is designed to provide SSH connectivity while minimizing the collection of personal information.

Your SSH credentials and saved host information are not sold to advertisers.

SSH Connections

SSH Now allows you to connect to SSH servers that you configure. Information such as hostnames, IP addresses, usernames, ports, connection settings, and credentials may be entered by you in order to use the app.

This information is used to provide SSH functionality. SSH traffic is exchanged between your device and the server you choose to connect to.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have authorization to access any server or system you connect to using SSH Now.

Saved Hosts and Credentials

SSH Now may store information necessary to provide saved-host and connection features on your device.

You should protect your device with appropriate security measures such as a device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID where available.

In-App Purchases

SSH Now may offer SSH Now Pro as a one-time in-app purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.

SSH Now does not directly receive or store your credit card number or other App Store payment credentials.

RevenueCat

SSH Now uses RevenueCat to help manage in-app purchases, determine whether SSH Now Pro has been unlocked, restore purchases, and provide purchase-related analytics.

RevenueCat may process purchase history and related identifiers necessary to provide these services. SSH Now does not use RevenueCat purchase information for third-party advertising.

RevenueCat's privacy practices are governed by RevenueCat's own privacy policy.

Apple

Purchases and App Store transactions are also subject to Apple's terms and privacy practices. Apple processes payments for in-app purchases.

Analytics and Advertising

SSH Now does not sell your SSH credentials, saved server information, or terminal contents to advertisers.

Purchase-related information may be processed by Apple and RevenueCat as necessary to provide in-app purchase functionality.

Third-Party Servers

SSH Now connects to servers specified by you. Those servers are operated by you or third parties and are outside the control of SSH Now.

The privacy, security, logging, and data-retention practices of those servers are governed by their respective owners and administrators.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by SSH Now. However, no application, device, network, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

SSH Now is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when SSH Now's features, services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
